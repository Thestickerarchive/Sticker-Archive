The Street Sticker Archive is a project created by an unknown individual, who goes by the name of Steven.

It was born out of a love of stickers and archival work, and a need for a comprehensive database for stickers found across the globe of all kinds.

However, Sport stickers and political stickers have not been included due to the use of AI in sport stickers (and not wanting to be affiliated with any teams, as rivals are not wanted) and the contriversional nature of political stickers.
Despite this, there is still a large amount of stickers in the archive, which is updated as regularly as possible.

If there are any concerns or you have some information about a particular creator who is listed as unknown, then please feel free to email streetstickerarchiv@outlook.com

Find us on Instagram, and enjoy the site!
